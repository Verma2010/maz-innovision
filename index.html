MAZ International Green Tech Drive

As a crucial prelude and core sustainability pillar of MAZ InnoVision 2026, our student leaders spearheaded the Green Tech Drive on Wednesday, 16 September 2026 at MAZ International School PJ. Timed around Malaysia Day, this student-led initiative mobilized the entire school community to take responsibility for electronic waste (e-waste) management and environmental preservation.

As the global move towards a digital world accelerates, electronic consumption increases rapidly. MAZ International is actively moving towards a greener future by leveraging technology to mitigate the environmental impact of digital transition. By keeping students at the forefront of this movement, the Green Tech Drive proves that technological advancement and environmental sustainability can go hand-in-hand.

Green Tech Drive Student Leaders & Faculty: E-waste donation drive team at MAZ International School. 16 Sept 2026
Awareness Presentation: Students presenting research on global e-waste hazards to encourage active recycling. Education
Interactive Commitment Wall: Students and guardians leaving signed pledges for circular tech habits. Pledge Wall

Empowering Community Change Through Interactive Advocacy

To maximize community outreach and shift environmental mindsets, student organizers structured the Green Tech Drive into dedicated educational and interactive commitment modules:

📢 Student-Led Awareness Pitching

Students delivered engaging presentations to peers, teachers, and parents detailing the toxic hazards of landfill e-waste and the immense economic value of recovering precious rare-earth metals.

📝 The Digital Commitment Wall

A central feature of the drive was the Commitment Wall, where participants wrote personal eco-pledges—promising to rehome working electronics, recycle dead batteries safely, and practice circular technology consumption.

🌱 Active E-Waste Collection Mobilization

By pairing educational awareness with a frictionless drop-off system, students encouraged families to clear out household e-waste, resulting in a substantial batch of collected hardware for DOE-compliant processing.

Completed Drive · 16 Sept 2026

Student-Led Digital Sustainability

With students at the forefront, teams designed interactive educational workshops for parents and guardians. They showcased how digital tracking and circular tech practices reduce e-waste hazards, recover precious metals, and align with global digital sustainability goals.

DOE-Approved Management

Digital Management & Responsible Recycling

All collected e-waste was inventoried digitally and sorted by student volunteers in designated collection rooms before being handed over to a Department of Environment (DOE)-approved organization for compliant recycling.

Key Highlights of the Greener Future Drive

📱 E-Waste & Digital Literacy

Educating the community on responsible technology usage and proper recycling of modern digital hardware.

📝 Digital Commitment Wall

Parents and students made personal sustainability pledges on an interactive board to reinforce long-term circular tech habits.

♻️ Student-Led Sorting

Systematic sorting and inventorying of donated devices by student leaders prior to DOE-compliant handling.

"Powering a Greener Digital World — Led by Students."

The Green Tech Drive demonstrated how MAZ International combines technological transition with eco-consciousness, putting students at the helm to drive meaningful, sustainable change for Malaysia and the digital world.

Digital Experience Pod — Senior Citizen Tech & AI Literacy

The Digital Experience Pod was a transformative community outreach initiative conceived and executed under MAZ InnoVision. Recognizing that the rapid transition into a digital world can leave older generations behind, student leaders took proactive initiative to bridge the digital divide by empowering senior citizens with vital technological skills and responsible AI usage.

Through interactive talks, structured presentations, informative infographics, and compassionate one-on-one volunteering, MAZ International School students personally guided senior citizens through every step of modern mobile navigation and everyday AI tools.

Digital Experience Pod Initiative: Student volunteers empowering senior citizens through digital literacy and practical AI training. Community Outreach

Two-Part Execution Model

To ensure deep engagement and max community impact, the Digital Experience Pod was executed in two distinct, coordinated phases:

Part 1 · School Campus Workshops

Part 1: MAZ International School PJ Campus

Hosted within the MAZ International School PJ Campus, students welcomed senior citizens into specialized workshop pods equipped with digital screens and mobile devices. Here, students delivered engaging keynote talks and presentations on mobile operating basics, online safety, scam prevention, and basic app navigation.

Part 2 · On-Site Centre Outreach

Part 2: Senior Citizen Centre Outreach

Student volunteer teams traveled directly to the local Senior Citizen Centre to deliver hands-on, personalized instruction in a comfortable, familiar environment. Students set up interactive help stations, providing tailored one-on-one coaching for individual device queries and real-world AI applications.

Student Presentations, Talks & Community Mentorship

Student Presentations & Talks: Students delivering structured talks on mobile functions and smartphone basics.
Digital Informatics & Visuals: Presenting clear, large-font infographics detailing mobile settings and communication apps.
Smartphone Basics: Patiently teaching foundational functions, messaging, and system settings.
Responsible AI Use: Guiding seniors on how AI tools can assist with daily queries and tasks safely.
Hands-On Guidance: Assisting seniors step-by-step through practical device exercises.
Senior Centre Outreach: Direct engagement and interactive support at the Senior Citizen Centre.
Interactive Q&A: Answering personal technology questions and resolving mobile issues.
Digital Inclusion: Fostering cross-generational connections through shared learning and technology.

What Senior Citizens Learned

The curriculum designed by MAZ students focused on practical, immediate digital skills that build confidence and independence:

📱 Essential Mobile Functions

Mastering screen magnification, Wi-Fi configuration, contact management, WhatsApp messaging, video calling, and digital scam awareness.

🤖 Responsible AI in Daily Life

Demonstrating how speech-to-text, voice assistants, and beginner AI tools can summarize information, answer questions, and assist in daily activities safely.

🔒 Cyber Safety & Trust

Teaching red flags for online scams, password hygiene, verifying digital sources, and ensuring confidential personal data stays protected.

Student Innovation Projects Showcase

Explore the innovative projects engineered by student teams for the digital expo. Positioned at the forefront of digital transformation, young minds spark innovation by addressing real-world challenges across assistive technology, mental health, eco-preservation, neural interfaces, flood defense, and cultural heritage.

Walk360

An AI-powered smart stick designed to assist visually impaired individuals in Malaysia with real-time hazard detection, vibration or audio navigation alerts, and emergency eSIM connectivity. It addresses complex technical challenges such as low-latency video feed processing and modem integration, driving direct social impact in support of SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities.

AI Hazard Detection eSIM Connectivity SDG 10 Assistive Tech

CarryMate

An innovative solution aimed at addressing mental health challenges like stress, anxiety, and loneliness among students. Key features include advanced AI integration, a huggable physical design with a built-in breathing module and warmth simulation, integrated solar panels, and interactive supportive communication.

Mental Health Tech Breathing Module Warmth Simulation Solar Powered

Aqua Sweep (by Ai Scapers)

Designed by team Ai Scapers, Aqua Sweep is an AI-powered floating net system created to detect, capture, and remove plastic waste from rivers using real-time AI image recognition techniques, protecting aquatic ecosystems before waste reaches the ocean.

AI Image Recognition River Cleaning Environmental Robotics

NeuroGuard

An innovative wearable technology idea featuring a revolutionary live search bar that delivers information directly to the brain—enabling users to receive search results as enhanced thoughts or insights while completely bypassing the need for screens or audio interfaces.

Neural Interface Live Direct Search Screenless Tech

Team Best — AI-Driven Flood Gate

An AI-driven automated flood gate that analyzes real-time satellite radar and local IoT sensors to predict imminent flood crests. Utilizing predictive machine learning models, it automatically opens or closes the barrier to actively protect urban zones while maintaining natural ecological water flow.

Predictive ML IoT Radar Sensors Flood Control

Team Kuih — AI Culinary Recognition Tool

An AI-powered tool built using machine learning that instantly identifies traditional Malaysian kuih from just an image. It helps users easily recognize look-alike snacks, offering a fun and innovative way for the next generation to connect with their rich cultural heritage.

Computer Vision Malaysian Culture Snack Classification
