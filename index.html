MAZ International Green Tech Drive

As a crucial prelude and core sustainability pillar of MAZ InnoVision 2026, our student leaders spearheaded the Green Tech Drive on Wednesday, 16 September 2026 at MAZ International School PJ. Timed around Malaysia Day, this student-led initiative mobilized the entire school community to take responsibility for responsible electronic waste (e-waste) management and environmental preservation.

Completed Drive · 16 Sept 2026

Student-Led Awareness & Action

Students designed and facilitated one-hour interactive sessions for parents and guardians, teaching the value of recovering precious materials from unused electronics, reducing e-waste landfill hazards, and adopting sustainable tech disposal habits.

DOE-Approved Management

Responsible E-Waste Handling

All collected e-waste was sorted by student volunteers in designated collection rooms and handed over to a Department of Environment (DOE)-approved organization to ensure 100% compliant, environmentally responsible recycling and processing.

Key Highlights of the Drive

📱 E-Waste Education

Interactive sessions educating parents on the hazardous materials inside obsolete electronics and the necessity of material recovery.

📝 Commitment Wall

Parents and students made personal sustainability pledges on our interactive wall to promote long-term circular tech habits.

♻️ Safe Collection Room

Systematic sorting and inventorying of donated devices by student volunteers prior to DOE-compliant handling.

"Let's Make a Difference — One Device at a Time."

The Green Tech Drive demonstrated how student-led initiatives can inspire an entire community to protect Malaysia's environment while underscoring core values of social responsibility, environmental stewardship, and proactive leadership.
