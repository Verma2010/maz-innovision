About MAZ International School

Petaling Jaya Campus — academic foundations behind the expo.

The Petaling Jaya (PJ) Campus of MAZ International School is renowned for fostering academic rigor, creative expression, and technological competence. Our secondary curriculum places heavy emphasis on practical computer science, problem-solving, and independent student research.
