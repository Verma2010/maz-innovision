Technology & The Move Towards a Digital World

In the modern world, technology is no longer merely an auxiliary tool—it is the bedrock of modern communication, industry, and global progress. The ongoing global transition toward a digital world demands rapid adaptation, analytical thinking, and fluency with emerging systems like artificial intelligence, IoT, and cloud computing.

To thrive in this digital era, students must be positioned at the forefront of technological change. By putting students in the driver's seat of technology development, the MAZ InnoVision program ensures that young minds do not just consume digital content, but actively engineer tools, automate workflows, and spark groundbreaking innovations that address human and environmental needs.

MAZ International Green Tech Drive

As a crucial prelude and core sustainability pillar of MAZ InnoVision 2026, our student leaders spearheaded the Green Tech Drive on Wednesday, 16 September 2026 at MAZ International School PJ. Timed around Malaysia Day, this student-led initiative mobilized the entire school community to take responsibility for electronic waste (e-waste) management and environmental preservation.

As the global move towards a digital world accelerates, electronic consumption increases rapidly. MAZ International is actively moving towards a greener future by leveraging technology to mitigate the environmental impact of digital transition. By keeping students at the forefront of this movement, the Green Tech Drive proves that technological advancement and environmental sustainability can go hand-in-hand.
